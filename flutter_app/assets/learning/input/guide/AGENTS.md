# Guide Content Instructions

## Scope
- Active numbered guide articles for the Flutter client.
- Keep durable guide content here; no drafts, archives, or temporary split files.

## File Rules
- Use stable numbered filenames such as `23_basic_prepositions.md`; do not renumber or rename unless requested.
- Use UTF-8, start with a Markdown heading, and avoid empty files.
- The first heading is the displayed title.
- Use simple Markdown only: headings, paragraphs, and bullets; no HTML, tables, or complex Markdown.

## Writing Rules
- Write clear, natural Ukrainian for a learner using the app as a reference, not a lesson script.
- Keep one central topic per file; split broad topics into tight subsections.
- Lead with the rule, contrast, or model, then give short examples and common mistakes where useful.
- Prefer short sections, short paragraphs, and simple bullet lists.
- Keep Hebrew, transliteration, and Ukrainian glosses consistent inside one file.
- Explain grammar terms in everyday language.
- Sound like a seasoned textbook teacher: concise, human, professional, patient.
- Favor correctness, natural phrasing, and retrievable explanations over flourish or long paraphrase.
- Call out Ukrainian-to-Hebrew interference when it prevents mistakes.

## Avoid
- Generic AI explainer tone, translation-engine phrasing, note dumps, or interchangeable paragraphs.
- Stiff meta-phrases, repeated framing formulas, inflated textbook wording, marketing tone, exaggerated friendliness, and direct calques when simpler Ukrainian exists.
- Full lesson flows, drills, homework, spaced repetition advice, or mini-dialogues unless requested.

## Notes
- Helper files like this `AGENTS.md` are not lessons.
- Stay aligned with neighboring guide files in tone and difficulty.
