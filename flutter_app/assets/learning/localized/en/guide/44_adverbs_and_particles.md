# Adverbs and particles: already, still, always, only

In real speech, short words such as “already,” “still,” “always,” “only,” “almost,” “also,” and “very” often matter as much as verbs. They set time, limitation, addition, focus, or degree inside one phrase.

Useful groups include time (כבר, עוד, עדיין), frequency (תמיד, לפעמים, אף פעם), limitation/addition (רק, גם), focus (כמעט, דווקא), and intensity (מאוד, ממש, די, כל כך, בקושי).

## Time and frequency

- כְּבָר – already: אֲנִי כְּבָר בַּבַּיִת – I am already at home
- עוֹד – still; another: אֲנִי עוֹד עוֹבֵד – I am still working; הִיא רוֹצָה עוֹד קָפֶה – she wants more coffee
- עֲדַיִן – still; yet: אֲנִי עֲדַיִן לוֹמֵד – I am still studying
- תָּמִיד – always: הוּא תָּמִיד בָּא בַּזְּמַן – he always arrives on time
- לִפְעָמִים – sometimes: לִפְעָמִים זֶה קָשֶׁה – sometimes it is difficult
- אַף פַּעַם לֹא – never: הִיא אַף פַּעַם לֹא מְאַחֶרֶת – she is never late

Remember the contrast: כבר means “already,” עוד means “still / more,” and עדיין means “still / yet.”

## Limitation and addition

- רַק – only: אֲנִי רַק שׁוֹאֵל – I am only asking; הוּא עוֹבֵד רַק בָּעֶרֶב – he works only in the evening
- גַּם – also: אֲנִי גַּם רוֹצֶה – I also want; הִיא גַּם לוֹמֶדֶת וְגַם עוֹבֶדֶת – she both studies and works

Place these words close to the item they qualify.

## Focus and degree

- כִּמְעַט – almost: אֲנִי כִּמְעַט מוּכָן – I am almost ready
- דַּוְקָא – specifically; actually; just: דַּוְקָא הַיּוֹם אֵין זְמַן – it is precisely today that there is no time
- מְאוֹד – very: הוּא מְאוֹד עָיֵף – he is very tired
- מַמָּשׁ – really; genuinely: זֶה מַמָּשׁ נָכוֹן – that is really true
- דַּי – quite; enough: הַסֵּפֶר דַּי טוֹב – the book is quite good
- כָּל כָּךְ – so; that much: הִיא כָּל כָּךְ שְׂמֵחָה – she is so happy
- בְּקֹשִׁי – barely: בְּקֹשִׁי הִצְלַחְתִּי לִישֹׁן – I could barely fall asleep

## Common mistakes

- Do not confuse כבר, עוד, and עדיין.
- Learn אַף פַּעַם לֹא as one ready-made negative block.
- Keep רק and גם near the word they limit or add.
- Learn דווקא in whole sentences; its shade is more important than a single fixed translation.
- Both מאוד and ממש intensify, but ממש adds a stronger sense of “really.”
