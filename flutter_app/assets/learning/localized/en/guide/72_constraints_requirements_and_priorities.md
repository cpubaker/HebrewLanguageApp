# Constraints, requirements, and priorities

At this level, you often need to do more than describe a situation. You need to set clear boundaries: what is mandatory, desirable, realistic, unrealistic, postponable, and the priority right now. Hebrew has a set of live patterns for this: חייב, צריך, אפשר, אי אפשר, אין טעם, העדיפות היא..., and העיקר הוא....

This topic is especially useful because it teaches you not simply to name requirements, but to put them in order. These patterns let language clearly hold boundaries, realism, and priority.

It is not only about “possible / impossible.” Often you need to separate a situation into several levels:

- what is mandatory;
- what is desirable;
- what is recommended;
- what is technically possible;
- what is realistic in the circumstances;
- what matters more than anything else right now.

Once these levels are clear, the conversation stops being vague.

In practice, the following patterns are especially common:

- חייב / חייבת... – must; absolutely need to...
- הכרחי / חיוני – necessary; essential
- צריך / צריכה... – need to...
- מומלץ – recommended
- אפשר... – possible; can...
- אי אפשר... / בלתי אפשרי – impossible; cannot...
- אין טעם... – there is no point...
- אין לי ברירה – I have no choice
- דחוף – urgent
- העדיפות היא... – the priority is...
- העיקר הוא... – the main thing is...

## Naming what is mandatory

חייב is stronger than צריך and sounds more forceful.

- אֲנַחְנוּ חַיָּבִים לְסַיֵּם הַיּוֹם – anakhnu khayavim lesayem ha-yom – we must finish today
- אַתְּ חַיֶּבֶת לִבְדֹּק אֶת זֶה שׁוּב – at khayevet livdok et ze shuv – you must check this again
- בַּמִּקְרֶה הַזֶּה חַיָּבִים אִשּׁוּר מֵרֹאשׁ – ba-mikre ha-ze khayavim ishur me-rosh – in this case, prior approval is mandatory

הכרחי or חיוני can sound even stronger when something is essential or cannot be done without.

- זֶה הֶכְרֵחִי לְהַצְלָחָה שֶׁל הַפְּרוֹיֶקְט – ze hekrekhi le-hatslakha shel ha-proyekt – this is necessary for the success of the project
- חִיּוּנִי שֶׁכֻּלָּם יָבִינוּ אֶת הַשִּׁנּוּי – khiyuni she-kulam yavinu et ha-shinui – it is essential that everyone understands the change

## Naming what is simply needed

צריך is more neutral and practical.

- צָרִיךְ עוֹד זְמַן – tsarikh od zman – more time is needed
- אֲנַחְנוּ צְרִיכִים לַחֲשֹׁב עַל זֶה מֵחָדָשׁ – anakhnu tsrikhim lakhshov al ze me-khadash – we need to think this through again
- כָּרֶגַע צָרִיךְ פִּתָּרוֹן פָּשׁוּט – ka-rega tsarikh pitaron pashut – a simple solution is needed right now

## Saying something is recommended

This is a softer model: advice, not an obligation.

- מֻמְלָץ לְהַתְחִיל מֻקְדָּם – mumlats lehatkhil mukdam – it is recommended to start early
- מֻמְלָץ שֶׁכֻּלָּם יִקְרְאוּ אֶת הַמִּסְמָךְ – mumlats she-kulam yikre'u et ha-mismakh – it is recommended that everyone read the document
- לֹא מֻמְלָץ לִדְחוֹת אֶת זֶה – lo mumlats lidkhot et ze – it is not recommended to postpone this

## Talking about urgency

דחוף is an extremely frequent marker of priority in a work setting.

- זֶה דָּחוּף – ze dakhuf – this is urgent
- יֵשׁ לִי בַּקָּשָׁה דְּחוּפָה – yesh li bakasha dkhufa – I have an urgent request
- צָרִיךְ תְּשׁוּבָה דְּחוּפָה עַד הַצָּהֳרַיִם – tsarikh tshuva dkhufa ad ha-tsohorayim – an urgent answer is needed by noon
- זֶה לֹא דָּחוּף, אֲבָל כְּדַאי לְהַסְפִּיק הַשָּׁבוּעַ – ze lo dakhuf, aval kedai lehaspik ha-shavua – this is not urgent, but it would be good to finish it this week

## Talking about possibility and its limits

- אֶפְשָׁר לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת זֶה עַד מָחָר – efshar la'asot et ze ad makhar – this can be done by tomorrow
- אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְסַיֵּם הַכֹּל הַיּוֹם – i efshar lesayem ha-kol ha-yom – it is impossible to finish everything today
- כָּרֶגַע אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְהִתְחַיֵּב לְזֶה – ka-rega i efshar lehitkhayev la-ze – it is impossible to commit to this right now
- בִּלְתִּי אֶפְשָׁרִי לְהַסְפִּיק לְבַד – bilti efshari lehaspik levad – it is impossible to manage alone
- אֵין לָנוּ אֶפְשָׁרוּת לְהַרְחִיב אֶת הַתַּקְצִיב – ein lanu efsharut leharkhiv et ha-taktsiv – we do not have the option of expanding the budget
- אֵין לִי בְּרֵרָה – ein li brera – I have no choice

## Saying something makes no sense

- אֵין טַעַם לְהַתְחִיל בְּלִי כָּל הַמֵּידָע – ein ta'am lehatkhil bli kol ha-meida – there is no point in starting without all the information
- כָּרֶגַע אֵין טַעַם לָדוּן בָּזֶה שׁוּב – ka-rega ein ta'am ladun ba-ze shuv – there is no point discussing this again right now
- אִם אֵין תַּקְצִיב, אֵין טַעַם לְהַמְשִׁיךְ בַּכִּוּוּן הַזֶּה – im ein taktsiv, ein ta'am lehamshikh ba-kivun ha-ze – if there is no budget, there is no point continuing in this direction

## Highlighting a priority

Here, it matters not only to say what is needed, but to name what is more important than everything else.

- הָעֲדִיפוּת כָּרֶגַע הִיא יַצִּיבוּת – ha-adifut ka-rega hi yatsivut – stability is the priority right now
- הָעֲדִיפוּת שֶׁלָּנוּ הִיא לְסַיֵּם אֶת הַשָּׁלָב הָרִאשׁוֹן – ha-adifut shelanu hi lesayem et ha-shlav ha-rishon – our priority is to finish the first stage
- הָעִיקָּר הוּא לֹא לְמַהֵר – ha-ikar hu lo lemaher – the main thing is not to hurry
- הַדָּבָר הֲכִי חָשׁוּב עַכְשָׁו הוּא תֵּאוּם בֵּין כֻּלָּם – ha-davar ha-khi khashuv akhshav hu te'um bein kulam – the most important thing right now is coordination among everyone

## Combining a requirement, constraint, and choice

- אֲנַחְנוּ צְרִיכִים פִּתָּרוֹן מָהִיר, אֲבָל אִי אֶפְשָׁר לִפְגֹּעַ בָּאֵיכוּת – anakhnu tsrikhim pitaron mahir, aval i efshar lifgoa ba-ekhut – we need a quick solution, but we cannot compromise quality
- חַיָּבִים לִקְבֹּעַ סֵדֶר עֲדִיפֻיּוֹת, כִּי אֵין זְמַן לְהַכֹּל – khayavim likboa seder adifuyot, ki ein zman la-kol – we must set priorities because there is not time for everything
- מִבְּחִינָתִי, הָעִיקָּר הוּא לִמְצֹא פִּתָּרוֹן רֵאָלִי – mi-bkhinati, ha-ikar hu limtso pitaron re'ali – as far as I am concerned, the main thing is to find a realistic solution

## Compare

- צָרִיךְ לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת זֶה – this needs to be done
- חַיָּבִים לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת זֶה הַיּוֹם – this must be done today
- מֻמְלָץ לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת זֶה הַשָּׁבוּעַ – it is recommended to do this this week

- אֶפְשָׁר – it is possible
- טֶכְנִית אֶפְשָׁר, אֲבָל כָּרֶגַע זֶה לֹא רֵאָלִי – technically it is possible, but it is not realistic right now

- זֶה חָשׁוּב – this is important
- זֶה חָשׁוּב, אֲבָל כָּרֶגַע זוֹ לֹא הָעֲדִיפוּת – this is important, but it is not the priority right now

## How this sounds in live speech

In natural Hebrew, limits and priorities often do not sound like a dry management list, but use short living formulas:

- צָרִיךְ...
- אִי אֶפְשָׁר...
- אֵין טַעַם...
- דָּחוּף
- הָעֲדִיפוּת הִיא...
- הָעִיקָּר הוּא...

They make it possible to outline the boundaries of a conversation quickly.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing up חייב and צריך. חייב is stronger. Using it too often makes speech sound harsher than necessary.
- Not distinguishing degrees of requirement. חיוני / חייב / צריך / מומלץ are four different gradations. If everything is called חייב, nuance disappears.
- Confusing what is possible with what is realistic. Sometimes something is אֶפְשָׁר, but in real conditions it is still not the best option. Say that separately.
- Not naming the priority directly. It is often no longer enough to say that “everything is important.” Show what matters most now.
- Rejecting an option without explanation. אֵין טַעַם... often sounds better when it is clear afterward why there is no point.
