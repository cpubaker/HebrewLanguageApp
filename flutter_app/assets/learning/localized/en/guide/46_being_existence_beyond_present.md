# Being and existence: היה, יהיה, יש, אין

In the Hebrew present tense, a separate word for “to be” is often unnecessary: אֲנִי בַּבַּיִת, הִיא עֲיֵפָה. In the past and future, however, forms of היה are needed. For “there is / there is no” and “I have / I do not have,” bare יש / אין work in the present only; past and future use forms of היה too.

Keep two groups separate:

- state or location: I am at home, she is ready, we were there;
- existence or possession: there is time, there was no water, I will have a book.

Useful pattern:

- present state: אֲנִי בַּבַּיִת – I am at home
- past state: הָיִיתִי בַּבַּיִת – I was at home
- future state: אֶהְיֶה בַּבַּיִת – I will be at home
- present existence: יֵשׁ זְמַן – there is time
- past existence: הָיָה זְמַן – there was time
- future existence: יִהְיֶה זְמַן – there will be time
- possession: יֵשׁ / הָיָה / יִהְיֶה לִי זְמַן – I have / had / will have time

## Common forms

Past: הָיִיתִי – I was; הָיִיתָ / הָיִיתְ – you were; הָיָה / הָיְתָה – he / she was; הָיִינוּ – we were; הָיוּ – they were.

Future: אֶהְיֶה – I will be; תִּהְיֶה / תִּהְיִי – you will be; יִהְיֶה – he will be; נִהְיֶה – we will be; יִהְיוּ – they will be.

## Examples

- הִיא הָיְתָה עֲיֵפָה – she was tired
- נִהְיֶה שָׁם בַּזְּמַן – we will be there on time
- יֵשׁ פֹּה מָקוֹם – there is room here
- אֵין תְּשׁוּבָה עֲדַיִן – there is no answer yet
- לֹא הָיָה זְמַן – there was no time
- יִהְיֶה זְמַן מָחָר – there will be time tomorrow

## Common mistake

Do not use bare יש / אין for past or future existence. Use forms of היה: הָיָה זְמַן / לֹא הָיָה זְמַן / יִהְיֶה זְמַן.
