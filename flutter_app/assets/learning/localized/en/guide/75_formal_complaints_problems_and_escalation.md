# Formal complaints, problems, and escalation

Sometimes you need to do more than ask a question. You need to describe a problem clearly and calmly, submit a complaint, request a check or resolution, and sometimes move to a more official tone without becoming rude. Hebrew has patterns for this, such as ברצוני לציין ש..., נתקלתי בבעיה, אבקש לבדוק, לא קיבלתי מענה, המצב אינו תקין, and אני מבקש טיפול בהקדם.

The strength of this language lies not in emotion, but in structure. Calm, precise wording makes a complaint serious without allowing it to become aggressive.

A formal complaint is not an emotional outburst, but a structured message:

- what happened;
- why it is a problem;
- what has already been done;
- when you expect a response;
- what you expect to happen next.

The following patterns are especially common:

- בִּרְצוֹנִי לְצַיֵּן שֶׁ... – I would like to note that...
- נִתְקַלְתִּי בִּבְעָיָה... – I encountered a problem...
- לֹא קִבַּלְתִּי מַעֲנֶה – I did not receive a reply
- אֲבַקֵּשׁ לִבְדֹּק אֶת הַנּוֹשֵׂא – I kindly request that you examine the matter
- הַמַּצָּב אֵינוֹ תָּקִין – the situation is unacceptable
- אֲנִי מְבַקֵּשׁ טִפּוּל בְּהֶקְדֵּם – I request prompt handling
- אִם לֹא יִמָּצֵא פִּתָּרוֹן... – if no solution is found...
- מִסְפַּר פְּנִיָּה – inquiry or case number
- תּוֹךְ X יָמִים – within X days
- לִידִיעַתְכֶם – for your information (FYI)
- לְמַעַן הָסֵר סָפֵק – for the avoidance of doubt

## Naming the problem calmly

The opening should be clear and free of unnecessary emotion.

- נִתְקַלְתִּי בִּבְעָיָה בַּחִיּוּב הָאַחֲרוֹן – nitkalti bi-ve'aya ba-khiyuv ha-akharon – I encountered a problem with the latest charge
- בִּרְצוֹנִי לְצַיֵּן שֶׁהַהַזְמָנָה טֶרֶם הִגִּיעָה – bi-rtsoni letsayen she-ha-hazmana terem higi'a – I would like to note that the order has still not arrived
- לְצַעֲרִי, הַשֵּׁרוּת שֶׁקִּבַּלְתִּי לֹא הָיָה מַסְפִּיק – le-tsa'ari, ha-sherut she-kibalti lo haya maspik – unfortunately, the service I received was inadequate
- הִתְגַּלְּתָה תַּקָּלָה בַּמַּעֲרֶכֶת – hitgalta takala ba-ma'arekhet – a fault was discovered in the system

## Describing the problem specifically

Specific detail is important here.

- קִבַּלְתִּי מוּצָר שׁוֹנֶה מִמַּה שֶׁהֻזְמַן – kibalti mutsar shone mi-ma she-huzman – I received a product different from the one ordered
- חֻיַּבְתִּי פַּעֲמַיִם עַל אוֹתָהּ הַזְמָנָה – khuyavti pa'amayim al ota ha-hazmana – I was charged twice for the same order
- לֹא קִבַּלְתִּי מַעֲנֶה לַפְּנִיָּה הַקּוֹדֶמֶת שֶׁלִּי – lo kibalti ma'ane la-pniya ha-kodemet sheli – I did not receive an answer to my previous inquiry
- הַמֵּידָע שֶׁנִּמְסַר לִי לֹא הָיָה מְדֻיָּק – ha-meida she-nimsar li lo haya meduyak – the information given to me was inaccurate

## Linking to a case number

In service companies and institutions, every inquiry usually receives a number. It is very helpful to state this number in a follow-up email or complaint: it saves time and shows that you are tracking the case.

- מִסְפַּר הַפְּנִיָּה שֶׁלִּי הוּא 12345 – mispar ha-pniya sheli hu 12345 – my case number is 12345
- בְּהֶמְשֵׁךְ לְפְנִיָּה מִסְפַּר 4321 – be-hemshekh le-pniya mispar 4321 – further to inquiry number 4321
- אֲבַקֵּשׁ לְהִתְעַדְכֵּן בִּסְטָטוּס פְּנִיָּה מִסְפַּר 999 – avakesh lehit'aden be-status pniya mispar 999 – I kindly request an update on the status of case number 999

## Requesting a check or action

This is the central part of such an inquiry.

- אֲבַקֵּשׁ לִבְדֹּק אֶת הַנּוֹשֵׂא בְּהֶקְדֵּם – avakesh livdok et ha-nose be-hekdem – I kindly request that you examine the matter as soon as possible
- אֶשְׂמַח אִם תּוּכְלוּ לַחֲזֹר אֵלַי עִם עִדְכּוּן – esmakh im tukhlu lakhzor elai im idkun – I would appreciate it if you could get back to me with an update
- אֲנִי מְבַקֵּשׁ טִפּוּל מִיָּדִי בַּמִּקְרֶה הַזֶּה – ani mevakesh tipul miyadi ba-mikre ha-ze – in this case, I request immediate handling
- אֲבַקֵּשׁ לְקַבֵּל הֶחְזֵר מָלֵא – avakesh lekabel hekhzer male – I kindly request a full refund

## Setting a reply deadline

In a formal complaint, you often need to state the expected response time.

- אֲבַקֵּשׁ תְּשׁוּבָה תּוֹךְ 14 יָמִים – avakesh tshuva tokh 14 yamim – I kindly request a response within 14 days
- אֶשְׂמַח לְמַעֲנֶה תּוֹךְ שָׁבוּעַ – esmakh le-ma'ane tokh shavua – I would appreciate a reply within a week
- אֲבַקֵּשׁ הִתְיַחֲסוּת עַד סוֹף הַחֹדֶשׁ – avakesh hityakhasut ad sof ha-khodesh – I kindly request a response by the end of the month
- בְּמַעֲרֶכֶת שֵׁרוּת לָקוֹחוֹת מְקֻבָּל לְהָגִיב תּוֹךְ 3 יְמֵי עֲסָקִים – be-ma'arekhet sherut lakokhot mekubal lehagiv tokh 3 yemei asakim – in customer service, it is customary to respond within three business days

A 14-day reply period is often requested in formal complaints; applicable deadlines vary by organisation and matter.

## Raising the tone without rudeness

Sometimes you need to show that the problem is serious, but without becoming aggressive.

- הַמַּצָּב הַזֶּה אֵינוֹ תָּקִין – ha-matsav ha-ze eino takin – this situation is unacceptable
- מִבְּחִינָתִי, זֶה לֹא מְקֻבָּל – mi-bkhinati, ze lo mekubal – as far as I am concerned, this is unacceptable
- מְדֻבָּר בְּעִכּוּב מַשְׁמָעוּתִי – medubar be-ikuv mashma'uti – this is a significant delay
- לֹא נִתָּן לְהַמְשִׁיךְ כָּךְ – lo nitan lehamshikh kakh – it cannot continue like this
- לְמַעַן הָסֵר סָפֵק, מְדֻבָּר בְּאוֹתוֹ הַחִיּוּב הַשָּׁגוּי שֶׁעָלָיו דֻּוַּח – le-ma'an haser safek, medubar be-oto ha-khiyuv ha-shaguy she-alav duvakh – for the avoidance of doubt, this is the same incorrect charge that was reported

## Referring to previous contacts

This is important when the problem has been continuing for more than one day.

- פָּנִיתִי אֲלֵיכֶם כְּבָר בְּשָׁבוּעַ שֶׁעָבַר – paniti aleikhem kvar be-shavua she-avar – I already contacted you last week
- זוֹ הַפַּעַם הַשְּׁנִיָּה שֶׁאֲנִי פּוֹנֶה בַּנּוֹשֵׂא הַזֶּה – zo ha-pa'am ha-shniya she-ani pone ba-nose ha-ze – this is the second time I am contacting you about this matter
- בְּהֶמְשֵׁךְ לַפְּנִיָּה הַקּוֹדֶמֶת, טֶרֶם קִבַּלְתִּי תְּשׁוּבָה – be-hemshekh la-pniya ha-kodemet, terem kibalti tshuva – further to the previous inquiry, I still have not received a reply
- לִידִיעַתְכֶם, פָּנִיתִי גַּם בְּעַל-פֶּה לַנְּצִיג בַּסְּנִיף – lidi'atkhem, paniti gam be-al-pe la-natsig ba-snif – for your information, I also contacted the representative in the branch in person

## Setting out the next step

The tone here should be official, but controlled.

- אִם לֹא יִמָּצֵא פִּתָּרוֹן, אֶצְטָרֵךְ לִפְנוֹת לְגוֹרֵם אַחֵר – im lo yimatsé pitaron, etstarekh lifnot le-gorem akher – if no solution is found, I will have to contact another authority
- אֲבַקֵּשׁ לְקַבֵּל תְּשׁוּבָה עַד סוֹף הַשָּׁבוּעַ – avakesh lekabel tshuva ad sof ha-shavua – I kindly request a reply by the end of the week
- אֲנִי מְצַפֶּה לְטִפּוּל בַּנּוֹשֵׂא בְּהֶקְדֵּם – ani metsape le-tipul ba-nose be-hekdem – I expect the matter to be handled as soon as possible
- בְּהֵיעָדֵר מַעֲנֶה, אֵאָלֵץ לִפְנוֹת לִמְמֻנֶּה – be-he'ader ma'ane, e'alets lifnot le-memune – in the absence of a reply, I will be forced to contact the person in charge

## Compare the tone

- יֵשׁ בְּעָיָה – there is a problem
- נִתְקַלְתִּי בִּבְעָיָה בַּחֶשְׁבּוֹן הָאַחֲרוֹן – I encountered a problem with the latest bill

- תְּטַפְּלוּ בְּזֶה – deal with this
- אֲבַקֵּשׁ לְטַפֵּל בַּנּוֹשֵׂא בְּהֶקְדֵּם – I kindly request that you handle the matter as soon as possible

- זֶה לֹא בְּסֵדֶר – this is not okay
- הַמַּצָּב הַזֶּה אֵינוֹ תָּקִין – this situation is unacceptable

## How this sounds in live use

In good formal Hebrew, a complaint should sound neither helpless nor aggressive. This order works most often:

- name the problem briefly;
- give one or two facts and the case number;
- refer to previous contact if there was any;
- set the expected reply time;
- state clearly what you expect next.

This structure gives the text weight without unnecessary harshness.

## Common mistakes

- Being too emotional instead of specific. In a formal complaint, facts and structure work better than emotional assessments.
- Describing the problem but not stating the request. After the problem, it should be clear what you want: a check, reply, refund, or update.
- Moving to a direct command. Usually, אֲבַקֵּשׁ..., אֶשְׂמַח אִם..., and אֲנִי מְצַפֶּה... sound stronger and more professional than an imperative.
- Threatening too early. Escalation should sound like the next formal step, not the first reaction.
- Not stating the case number and deadline. Without מִסְפַּר פְּנִיָּה and תּוֹךְ X יָמִים, a complaint remains in the general flow. These details substantially improve the chance of a quick response.
