# Cause, result, and contrast

At first, “because” and “but” are often enough. In live speech, however, you quickly need more precise links: “because of this,” “thanks to,” “therefore,” “despite,” “although,” “on the other hand,” and “instead.” These let you do more than connect sentences: they show the logic between them.

This topic becomes much easier when you focus not on a list of conjunctions, but on the kind of logical movement in the thought: why something happened, what follows from it, what happened despite an obstacle, or where the contrast lies.

The issue is not separate words but the type of link between two parts of an idea:

- cause, whether negative or positive;
- result;
- concession;
- contrast or compensation.

Four large groups are especially useful:

- cause: בגלל, בזכות, מפני ש, כי;
- result: לכן, בגלל זה;
- concession: למרות, למרות ש, אף ש;
- contrast and “on the other hand”: לעומת זאת, מצד שני, אבל.

## Cause: what exactly caused it

At first, many learners rely only on כי, but live speech often needs two more patterns.

- בגלל + a noun or short reason: a neutral or negative cause
- בזכות + a noun: a positive cause, “thanks to”
- מפני ש + a complete clause giving the cause

Examples:

- אֲנִי בַּבַּיִת בִּגְלַל הַגֶּשֶׁם – ani ba-bayit biglal ha-geshem – I am at home because of the rain
- הִיא מְאַחֶרֶת בִּגְלַל הַפְּקָק – hi me'akheret biglal ha-pkak – she is late because of the traffic jam
- הוּא נִשְׁאַר בַּבַּיִת מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהוּא חוֹלֶה – hu nish'ar ba-bayit mipnei she-hu khole – he is staying at home because he is ill
- הִצְלַחְנוּ בִּזְכוּת הָעֶזְרָה שֶׁלָּהּ – hitslakhnu bi-zkhut ha-ezra shela – we succeeded thanks to her help
- בִּזְכוּת הַתָּכְנִית, הַכֹּל עָבַר חָלָק – bi-zkhut ha-tokhnit, ha-kol avar khalak – thanks to the plan, everything went smoothly

The difference between בגלל and בזכות matters: בזכות has a positive colouring, “thanks to,” while בגלל is neutral or negative, “because of.” They are rarely confused in live speech.

## Result: what follows from it

Use these when you need to show the result rather than explain the cause:

- לכן – therefore; so
- בגלל זה – because of this; therefore

Examples:

- יֵשׁ פְּקָק, לָכֵן אֲנַחְנוּ מְאַחֲרִים – yesh pkak, lakhen anakhnu me'akhrim – there is a traffic jam, so we are late
- הִיא עֲיֵפָה, בִּגְלַל זֶה הִיא לֹא יוֹצֵאת – hi ayefa, biglal ze hi lo yotset – she is tired, so she is not going out
- אֵין זְמַן, לָכֵן נְדַבֵּר מָחָר – ein zman, lakhen nedaber makhar – there is no time, so we will talk tomorrow

## Concession: despite this

The language acknowledges an obstacle but shows that the action happens anyway.

- למרות + noun
- למרות ש + complete clause

Examples:

- לַמְרוֹת הָעֲיֵפוּת, אֲנִי עוֹבֵד – lamrot ha-ayefut, ani oved – despite the tiredness, I am working
- לַמְרוֹת הַגֶּשֶׁם, הֵם יָצְאוּ – lamrot ha-geshem, hem yats'u – despite the rain, they went out
- לַמְרוֹת שֶׁהוּא עָיֵף, הוּא מַמְשִׁיךְ לִלְמֹד – lamrot she-hu ayef, hu mamshikh lilmod – although he is tired, he continues studying

## Contrast and “on the other hand”

The English “but” or “on the other hand” does not always have one fixed equivalent.

- לעומת זאת – by contrast; on the other hand
- מצד שני – on the other hand
- אבל – but, when the contrast is simpler and more direct
- בסך הכל – all in all; overall, for a soft compromise

Examples:

- אֲנִי לֹא גָּר קָרוֹב, לְעֻמַּת זֹאת אֲנִי עוֹבֵד מֵהַבַּיִת – ani lo gar karov, le'umat zot ani oved me-ha-bayit – I do not live nearby; on the other hand, I work from home
- הוּא לֹא מָהִיר, מִצַּד שֵׁנִי הוּא מְאוֹד יְסוֹדִי – hu lo mahir, mi-tsad sheni hu me'od yesodi – he is not fast, but on the other hand he is very thorough
- אֵין לָנוּ הַרְבֵּה זְמַן, אֲבָל יֵשׁ לָנוּ תָּכְנִית טוֹבָה – ein lanu harbe zman, aval yesh lanu tokhnit tova – we do not have much time, but we have a good plan
- בְּסַךְ הַכֹּל זֶה לֹא נוֹרָא – be-sakh ha-kol ze lo nora – all in all, it is not so bad

## More formal options

In writing or a more official style, you may encounter forms that sound bookish in conversation:

- עַל אַף + noun – despite, more formal than למרות
- אַף שֶׁ + clause – although, more formal than למרות ש
- כְּתוֹצָאָה מִ + noun – as a result of
- בְּעִקְבוֹת + noun – following; as a result of an event

Examples:

- עַל אַף הַקְּשִׁיִּים, הֶחְלַטְנוּ לְהַמְשִׁיךְ – al af ha-kshayim, hekhlatnu lehamshikh – despite the difficulties, we decided to continue
- אַף שֶׁהוּא לֹא הִסְכִּים, הַפְּגִישָׁה הִתְקַיְּמָה – af she-hu lo hiskim, ha-pgisha hitkayma – although he did not agree, the meeting took place
- כְּתוֹצָאָה מֵהַשִּׁנּוּי, הַלּוּחַ הוּזַז – ke-totsa'a me-ha-shinui, ha-luakh huzaz – as a result of the change, the schedule was moved
- בְּעִקְבוֹת הַפְּגִישָׁה, נַעֲשׂוּ הַחְלָטוֹת – be-ikvot ha-pgisha, na'asu hakhlatot – following the meeting, decisions were made

For everyday speech, the simpler forms בגלל, לכן, and למרות ש are enough. Keep the formal forms for official writing.

## Feeling the difference

Compare:

- אֲנִי בַּבַּיִת כִּי יוֹרֵד גֶּשֶׁם – I am at home because it is raining
- אֲנִי בַּבַּיִת בִּגְלַל הַגֶּשֶׁם – I am at home because of the rain
- יוֹרֵד גֶּשֶׁם, לָכֵן אֲנִי בַּבַּיִת – it is raining, so I am at home
- לַמְרוֹת הַגֶּשֶׁם, אֲנִי יוֹצֵא – despite the rain, I am going out
- בִּזְכוּת הַגֶּשֶׁם, הָאֲוִיר נָקִי – thanks to the rain, the air is clean

The same facts can be linked in different ways, and each pattern shows a slightly different logic.

## Short pairs

- הוּא לֹא בָּא כִּי הוּא עוֹבֵד – he is not coming because he is working
- הוּא עוֹבֵד, לָכֵן הוּא לֹא בָּא – he is working, so he is not coming

- לַמְרוֹת הַגֶּשֶׁם, אֲנַחְנוּ הוֹלְכִים – despite the rain, we are going
- בִּגְלַל הַגֶּשֶׁם, אֲנַחְנוּ לֹא הוֹלְכִים – because of the rain, we are not going

- אֲנִי לֹא פָּנוּי הַיּוֹם, לְעֻמַּת זֹאת מָחָר כֵּן – I am not free today; tomorrow, on the other hand, I am
- אֲנִי לֹא פָּנוּי הַיּוֹם, אֲבָל מָחָר כֵּן – I am not free today, but I am tomorrow

- זֶה קָרָה בִּגְלַל הַטָּעוּת שֶׁלִּי – this happened because of my mistake
- זֶה קָרָה בִּזְכוּת הָעֶזְרָה שֶׁלָּךְ – this happened thanks to your help

## Common mistakes

- Using only כי for everything. כי is useful, but it does not cover result, concession, and contrast.
- Confusing cause and result. בגלל / בזכות show what caused something; לכן shows what follows from it.
- Looking for one magical translation for “but” or “on the other hand.” In live language, look at the intended meaning: simple contrast (אבל), compensation or contrast (לעומת זאת), or another side of the question (מצד שני).
- Building overly bookish chains where a simple living pattern is enough. Often short בִּגְלַל זֶה, לַמְרוֹת שֶׁ, or לְעֻמַּת זֹאת works better than a heavy construction.
- Confusing בגלל and בזכות. בזכות has a positive colouring, “thanks to.” For a neutral or negative cause use בגלל; if something good happened because of someone or something, use בזכות.
