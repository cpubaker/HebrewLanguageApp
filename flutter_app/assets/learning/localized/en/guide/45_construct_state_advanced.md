# Smikhut and the של construction

Smikhut and the של pattern can express similar ideas, but feel different in speech. Smikhut is usually shorter and more compact; של is clearer and easier in longer phrases.

Examples of smikhut:

- בֵּית סֵפֶר – beit sefer – school
- חֲדַר עֲבוֹדָה – khadar avoda – work room
- יוֹם הֻלֶּדֶת – yom huledet – birthday

Examples with של:

- הַחֶדֶר שֶׁל הַמּוֹרָה – ha-kheder shel ha-mora – the teacher’s room
- הַסֵּפֶר שֶׁל דָּנָה – ha-sefer shel Dana – Dana’s book
- הַחֲבֵרִים שֶׁל יוֹסִי – ha-khaverim shel Yosi – Yosi’s friends

Smikhut often sounds like a fixed name or a compact category, while של clearly shows possession.

## Definiteness in smikhut

This is a central rule: in smikhut, put the article ה on the final word, not the first. The first word becomes definite automatically because the whole group is one unit.

- בֵּית סֵפֶר – a school
- בֵּית הַסֵּפֶר – the school
- סֵפֶר יְלָדִים – a children’s book (a type)
- סֵפֶר הַיְלָדִים – the children’s book

Correct: בֵּית הַסֵּפֶר. Not *הַבַּיִת סֵפֶר or *הַבֵּית הַסֵּפֶר.

## Adjectives after smikhut

An adjective follows the whole smikhut block and agrees with its first word, the head noun:

- בֵּית הַסֵּפֶר הַחָדָשׁ – the new school
- סִפְרֵי הַיְלָדִים הַחֲדָשִׁים – the new children’s books

The adjective also takes the article because the whole phrase is definite.

## Choosing smikhut or של

Choose smikhut when the phrase is a fixed collocation, short, and easy to understand:

- בֵּית קָפֶה – café
- תַּחֲנַת אוֹטוֹבּוּס – bus stop
- חֲדַר אֹכֶל – dining room

Choose של when the group is long, the owner needs to be explicit, or smikhut would feel overloaded:

- הַתִּיק שֶׁל הַמּוֹרָה הַחֲדָשָׁה – the new teacher’s bag
- הַמַּחְשֵׁב שֶׁל הָאָח שֶׁלִּי – my brother’s computer

## Common mistakes

- Do not build overly long smikhut chains; use של for clarity.
- Do not use של in established names such as בֵּית סֵפֶר and יוֹם הֻלֶּדֶת.
- Distinguish category/name (often smikhut) from concrete possession (often של).
- Do not put the article on the first smikhut word.
- Do not put an adjective inside a smikhut group: say בֵּית הַסֵּפֶר הַחָדָשׁ, not *בֵּית הַחָדָשׁ הַסֵּפֶר.
